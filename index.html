My Story

At a young age, I lost my little sister when she was just only less than a year old. This really did shape my perspective of reality and did hurt me, bring me down to depression. It hurt me so much I was genuinely considering suicide, luckily I was stopped in time and I didn't. If you are considering leaving the lobby early, DON'T. If I did that 7 years ago, I wouldn't have met my girlfriend, my little brother, and have the platform I have today.
